Who I am

I work at the intersection of data engineering, analytics, and business strategy. Over the past four years I've built reporting pipelines in BigQuery, executive dashboards in Power BI, and data quality frameworks for e-commerce operations spanning multiple countries across Latin America.

Before that I spent nearly three years in digital marketing analytics at IPG Mediabrands, where I learned to translate campaign data into investment decisions — and, more importantly, to make stakeholders care about the numbers.
